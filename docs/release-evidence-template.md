## Release decision

State whether this is a preview or recommended build, supported configurations, known defects, exact limits, data-preservation steps, and why an earlier version may not be a safe rollback. Link sanitized public issues and reproducible fixtures. Do not label numerical tests or screenshots as wearer acceptance.
